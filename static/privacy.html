Privacy

A plain-language description of what this app does with data, exactly as it is built. There are no accounts, and the app never asks for your name or email address.

What is stored on the server

  • A random learner id. Your browser makes it the first time you visit. It is the only thing that links you to your history, so treat it like a password. It is not derived from you.
  • Your practice answers: which question, which option you picked, whether you marked it “sure” or “guessing”, the practice mode, how many seconds you took, and when.
  • Results you choose to report (an official exam result or a Microsoft practice-assessment score), with the date you give and the readiness estimate the app had at that time.
  • A cache of AI explanations keyed by question, never by person.

What is stored in your browser

Your learner id, questions you saved, your study-plan settings and ticked blocks, and any practice session in progress. This uses the browser’s local storage on this device. The app sets no cookies.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • The server’s own logs record a request id, the method, the page path, the status and how long it took. They do not record query strings (which is where your learner id travels) or your answers.
  • Your address is held in memory for a few minutes to apply rate limits and is not stored. The company hosting this site may keep its own network logs, including IP addresses.

AI explanations (optional)

If the operator has switched them on, pressing “Explain it simply” sends that question’s text, its answer options, the option you picked and the curated explanation to Anthropic’s API so it can write a plainer version. Your learner id and anything else about you are not sent.

How reported results are used

Reported exam results and practice-assessment scores are used in aggregate to check how closely the readiness estimate matches real outcomes. Individual results are not published.

Your choices

  • Take it with you: “Export my data” on the dashboard downloads your history as a file you can import on another browser.
  • Delete it: “Reset my data” permanently deletes your answers and reported results from the server.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data removes your learner id from this device. Export first if you want to keep your history, because without the id nobody can reach it again.

How long data is kept

Until you reset it or the operator removes it. Some free hosting plans erase the database whenever the service restarts or redeploys, so your history is not guaranteed to last; export a backup if it matters to you.
